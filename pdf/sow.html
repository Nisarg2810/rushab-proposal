The website What a traveller sees

  • Home with package search by destination, month, nights and budget
  • Package listing filtered by destination, length, budget, theme and departure city, sorted by price or duration
  • Destination pages, one per destination, each with its own packages and photos
  • Package page: gallery, named hotels with room type and meal plan, day wise itinerary with dates and photos per day, sightseeing, inclusions, exclusions, special notes, cancellation and payment policy
  • Booking box with travel date, rooms as well as adults and children, and a live per person and total price
  • Share this package button: WhatsApp, copy link and email
  • Download the package as a PDF, in exchange for a name, number and email, which becomes a lead
  • Guest photo gallery and an Instagram feed section, pulling your real traveller photos
  • Testimonials, approved by you before they appear
  • Social links in the header and footer
  • Flights, hotels, bus and insurance link out to your booking partner
  • Group tours, visa, about and contact pages, all editable
  • Mobile first, fast on 4G, built for Google with clean URLs and redirects from your current site

WhatsApp Where most enquiries will come from

  • Floating button on every page, labelled Chat With Expert
  • Short scripted chat first: destination, month, travellers, budget
  • Hands over to real WhatsApp with those answers already written in
  • Every new lead is pushed to your team on WhatsApp the moment it arrives, with the package and the number

Delivery 4 to 5 weeks

  • Week 1 Design sign off, database, project setup
  • Week 2 The API, logins and the different access rights
  • Week 3 The dashboard, so your team can start loading packages
  • Week 4 The website, enquiry flow, WhatsApp and lead capture
  • Week 5 Your packages moved across, training, testing, Google redirects, live

The dashboard dashboard.rushabtours.com

  • Package editor in eight tabs: overview, hotels, itinerary, sightseeing, inclusions, pricing, gallery and the Google listing. No code, no developer
  • Drag to reorder days and lines, duplicate last year's package to build this year's
  • Seasonal pricing by date range, with child rates
  • Media library with upload, crop and reuse across packages
  • Destinations, testimonials and the guest gallery, all managed here
  • Draft, preview and publish, so nothing goes live by accident
  • Admin and employee accounts with different rights: owner sees everything, manager edits packages and works leads, sales sees only leads, content sees only packages and photos

Leads and CRM Every enquiry, nothing lost

  • Captured: name, mobile, WhatsApp, email, package, travel date, rooms, adults, children and ages, budget and their note
  • Captured silently: whether the lead came from Google Ads, organic search, Instagram, a referral or the WhatsApp button, down to the campaign or search term
  • City, device, time on site, pages viewed and the package they looked at first
  • Pipeline: new, contacted, quoted, won, lost, with owner, follow up reminder and full history
  • Lead popup on package pages, sliding in from the right, once per visit
  • One tap to call, or to open WhatsApp with their name and package filled in
  • Reporting by month, package and source, so you know which ad actually paid
  • Export to Excel any time

Not included So there are no surprises

  • Flight and hotel booking inventory. These link out to your partner
  • Online payment and instant booking. Can be added later as a separate piece
  • Photography, videography and writing new package copy. We move and tidy what you have
  • Running your ad campaigns, although the tracking for them is built in
  • Third party fees: domain, hosting, and the official WhatsApp Business API if you want it later
